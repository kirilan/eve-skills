# supply-run: example playbook

Copy to `$(eve-skills operation playbook supply-run --path)`. Everything here is invented.

## Horizon and hauler
- Plan 4 days ahead. Carol Hauler flies a 6,000 m3 hauler; bulk goes by courier on `hub->home`.

## Where to buy
- Buy everything at Jita; the local structure market only for emergencies.

## Standing buy orders
- Keep bids only for inputs whose gap after the broker fee is over 3%; re-measure each visit.

## Arrival
- Inputs go to Carol Hauler's item hangar at Example Factory; datacores and decryptors to Example Lab.
