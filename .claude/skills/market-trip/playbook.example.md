# market-trip: example playbook

Copy to `$(eve-skills operation playbook market-trip --path)`. Everything here is invented.

## Seller and hubs
- Carol Hauler lists everything (`sites.home.seller`). Sell at Jita; the local market only up to a week of
  its own volume per item.

## Keep at home
- Never sell datacores, decryptors or the components we build with (`selling.keep`).

## Trips
- A sell run is worth it from about 2B of unlisted stock or an undercut stack worth three days of volume.
