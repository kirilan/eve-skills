# industry-jobs: example playbook

Copy to `$(eve-skills operation playbook industry-jobs --path)` and replace everything with your own
operation's rules. Everything here is invented. Rules in the playbook win over the skill's defaults.

## Where and what
- Default site `home` (a personal site): invent and copy at Example Lab, build at Example Factory.
- Lines we invent: Hobgoblin II, Damage Control II. Build out copies of anything else, invent no more.
- Never build: Large Example Rig II (loses money at the hub).

## Finish times and leftover runs
- Jobs should end 07-09 or 19-21 local time (`planning.finish_windows`).
- Run whole copies rather than leaving fewer than half the runs on a copy.

## Who builds and invents
- Alice Builder and Bob Inventor build everything; only Bob Inventor has the encryption skill to invent.
- Carol Hauler holds the pool and copies the T1 originals.

## Decryptors
- Drones: Augmentation. Modules: Parity (copies come out ME3).

## Passing stock
- Carol Hauler gives the whole stack to each builder in turn ("Deliver to"); each moves it from
  Deliveries to the item hangar, installs, and gives everything back.

## Reply format
- Install order first, then one table per activity. Always write the runs on each copy.

## Companion files
- `industry-jobs-history`: dated rankings and the reasons behind rules (read when a rule is questioned).
